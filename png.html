PNG Format Example

About This Image

This image shows a green leaf with a simple shape and clear edges. Leaf graphics can be used in nature websites, educational projects, and environmental posters.

Why PNG Format?

PNG is a good choice for this graphic because it preserves sharp edges and supports transparent backgrounds. Transparency allows the leaf to appear over different background colors without a solid rectangular background.
