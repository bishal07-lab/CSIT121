GIF Image Format

About This Image

This animated image shows a soccer ball moving across a soccer field. It represents the movement and action that make soccer an exciting sport.

Why GIF Format?

GIF is useful for this image because it supports simple animations. It can display multiple frames in one file, allowing the image to move without requiring a video player.
