About This Image

This photograph shows a soccer player running across the field while controlling the ball. It represents the speed, movement, and excitement of soccer.

Why JPG Format?

JPG is a good format for this photograph because it supports many colors and details. It also compresses photographs to smaller file sizes, making them easier to load on websites.
