Welcome to Soccer World!

Soccer is one of the most popular sports in the world. It brings people together through teamwork, competition, and passion for the game.

This website explores three common web image formats: JPG, PNG, and GIF. Each format has different features that make it useful for displaying soccer photographs, logos, and animations on websites.
