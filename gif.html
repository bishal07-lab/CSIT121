GIF Format Example

About This Image

This image represents a flower in a nature setting. An animated version can show movement, such as petals opening or a flower gently swaying. Animation can make a nature website more interesting.

Why GIF Format?

GIF supports simple animations by displaying a sequence of images. It works well for short animations with a limited number of colors, although it is less suitable for detailed photographs with many colors.
