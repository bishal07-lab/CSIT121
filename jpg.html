JPG Format Example

About This Image

This photograph shows a green forest filled with trees and plants. Sunlight shines through the trees and creates different shades of green. The scene shows the natural beauty of a forest.

Why JPG Format?

JPG is a good format for this photograph because it contains many colors and smooth transitions between light and dark areas. JPG compression can reduce the file size while keeping the photograph looking clear.
